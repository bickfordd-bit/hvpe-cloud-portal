## Persona 1: "Overwhelmed Owen" — Solo GovCon Consultant

### Demographics
- **Title**: Owner/Principal Consultant
- **Company Size**: 1-3 employees
- **Revenue**: $500K - $2M annually
- **Age**: 45-60
- **Location**: DC Metro area (Northern VA, MD)
- **Education**: Bachelor's + Security Clearance

### Professional Background
- Former government employee (GS-13 to GS-15)
- 20+ years in federal IT/consulting
- Started consulting firm after retirement
- Networks through LinkedIn, NCMA events

### Goals & Motivations
- **Primary Goal**: Win 3-5 contracts per year to sustain income
- **Secondary Goal**: Maintain work-life balance (no 80-hour weeks)
- **Aspiration**: Build reputation as domain expert, retire comfortably

### Pain Points
- Spends 40+ hours analyzing each RFP (too slow)
- Can't afford full-time proposal writer ($80K salary)
- Loses bids because proposal was late or non-compliant
- Doesn't know which RFPs are worth pursuing (wastes time on unwinnable)

### Current Workflow
1. Finds RFPs on SAM.gov (manually checks daily)
2. Downloads RFP PDF, reads 100+ pages by hand
3. Makes notes in Word doc (unstructured)
4. Googles past projects to find relevant experience
5. Writes proposal in Word (takes 60-80 hours)
6. Submits via email/SAM.gov upload

### Technology Profile
- **Tech Savvy**: Medium (uses Word, Excel, email)
- **Budget**: $500-1000/month for tools
- **Current Tools**: Microsoft 365, QuickBooks, LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- **Barriers**: Skeptical of AI, wants simple UI

### Buying Behavior
- **Research**: Googles "RFP analysis tool", asks peers in NCMA Slack
- **Decision Timeline**: 2-4 weeks (needs to see ROI quickly)
- **Decision Criteria**: Easy to use, affordable, saves time
- **Preferred Sales Motion**: Self-serve trial → monthly subscription

### How OPTR Helps
- **Before OPTR**: 40 hours to analyze RFP + write proposal
- **After OPTR**: 5 minutes to analyze + 20 hours to write (50% time savings)
- **ROI**: Save $8,000 per proposal (32 hours × $250/hr opportunity cost)
- **Win**: Bid on 2× more RFPs per year → $1M additional revenue potential

### Messaging That Resonates
- ✅ "Analyze RFPs in minutes, not days"
- ✅ "Know which RFPs to bid on (and which to skip)"
- ✅ "Never miss a requirement again"
- ❌ "Enterprise-grade AI platform" (too technical)
- ❌ "Integrates with Salesforce" (doesn't use CRM)

### Sales Playbook
1. **Outreach**: LinkedIn InMail referencing mutual NCMA connection
2. **Hook**: "I noticed you bid on [recent RFP]. Curious how long it took to analyze?"
3. **Demo**: Show live RFP upload → OPTR run → coverage report (5 min)
4. **Trial**: 14-day free trial with sample RFPs
5. **Close**: Monthly subscription ($800/month) with first month 50% off

## Persona 2: "Scaling Sarah" — Proposal Manager at Growing Contractor

### Demographics
- **Title**: Proposal Manager / Director of Business Development
- **Company Size**: 25-100 employees
- **Revenue**: $10M - $50M annually
- **Age**: 35-50
- **Location**: Major metro (DC, San Diego, Colorado Springs, Huntsville)
- **Education**: MBA or Bachelor's in relevant field

### Professional Background
- 10+ years in proposal management (APMP certified)
- Worked at large prime, moved to smaller firm for growth opportunity
- Manages team of 3-5 proposal writers
- Responsible for $50M+ annual pipeline

### Goals & Motivations
- **Primary Goal**: Increase win rate from 25% to 40%
- **Secondary Goal**: Reduce proposal costs (labor + consultants)
- **Aspiration**: Become VP of Business Development, grow company to $100M

### Pain Points
- Team is overwhelmed (responding to 50+ RFPs per year)
- Hiring more proposal writers is expensive ($80K each)
- Can't scale past performance library (1000+ Word docs, not searchable)
- Executives ask "Why didn't we bid on that?" but team was at capacity
- Manual compliance matrices take 10 hours per proposal

### Current Workflow
1. Receive RFP from BD team (usually 2-4 weeks before deadline)
2. Kickoff meeting (2 hours to assign sections)
3. Research past performance (3 hours searching SharePoint/email)
4. Draft outline and compliance matrix (6 hours)
5. Writers create content (40 hours total)
6. Reviews and revisions (20 hours)
7. Final production (8 hours)
8. **Total**: 77 hours per proposal × $100/hr = $7,700 cost

### Technology Profile
- **Tech Savvy**: High (uses Salesforce, Microsoft 365, project management tools)
- **Budget**: $5K-10K/month for proposal tools
- **Current Tools**: RFPIO (manual tagging), SharePoint, Salesforce, Loopio (content library)
- **Barriers**: Needs integration with existing systems, wants ROI proof

### Buying Behavior
- **Research**: Attends APMP conferences, follows proposal consultants on LinkedIn
- **Decision Timeline**: 3-6 months (needs executive buy-in and pilot)
- **Decision Criteria**: Integrations, scalability, proven ROI
- **Preferred Sales Motion**: Demo → 30-day pilot → annual contract

### How OPTR Helps
- **Before OPTR**: Team can handle 50 RFPs/year at capacity
- **After OPTR**: Team can handle 100 RFPs/year (2× throughput)
- **ROI**: Save 30 hours per proposal × 50 proposals × $100/hr = $150K annual savings
- **Win**: Increase win rate 25% → 35% = $5M additional revenue

### Messaging That Resonates
- ✅ "2× your proposal capacity without hiring"
- ✅ "AI-powered past performance search (no more SharePoint hunts)"
- ✅ "Automated compliance matrices in 2 minutes"
- ✅ "Integrates with Salesforce, RFPIO, and Microsoft 365"
- ❌ "Simple tool for solo consultants" (too small-scale)

### Sales Playbook
1. **Outreach**: Warm intro from APMP chapter president
2. **Discovery Call**: "Walk me through your current proposal process"
3. **Demo**: Show gap analysis + past performance search (15 min)
4. **Pilot**: 30 days, 3 live proposals, measure time savings
5. **Close**: Annual contract ($90K/year) with quarterly business reviews

## Persona 5: "Compliance Carol" — Contracts Manager at SMB Contractor

### Demographics
- **Title**: Contracts Manager / Compliance Officer
- **Company Size**: 10-50 employees
- **Revenue**: $5M - $25M annually
- **Age**: 40-55
- **Location**: Any (remote-friendly)
- **Education**: Bachelor's (often in business or legal studies)

### Professional Background
- Former government contracting officer (1102 series)
- 15+ years in federal contracts (FAR/DFARS expert)
- Ensures company stays compliant (DCAA audits, CPARS, etc.)
- Works closely with proposal and finance teams

### Goals & Motivations
- **Primary Goal**: Zero non-compliant proposals (avoid protests)
- **Secondary Goal**: Maintain perfect CPARS ratings (all "Exceptional")
- **Aspiration**: Become VP of Contracts, teach FAR courses

### Pain Points
- Manual compliance matrices take 8-12 hours per proposal
- Miss requirements → non-responsive proposals → wasted bid cost ($30K+)
- Executives rush proposals ("Just submit it!") → Carol has to fix errors
- No time to review every proposal (team submits 30+ per year)
- DCAA audits are stressful (need perfect documentation)

### Current Workflow
1. Receive final proposal draft 48 hours before deadline
2. Read RFP Section L/M (4 hours)
3. Create compliance matrix in Excel (6 hours)
4. Flag missing requirements, send back to writers (2 hours)
5. Re-review after fixes (2 hours)
6. **Total**: 14 hours per proposal (under time pressure)

### Technology Profile
- **Tech Savvy**: Medium (expert in Excel, learning AI tools)
- **Budget**: $500-2K/month for compliance tools
- **Current Tools**: Excel, Word, Adobe Acrobat, contract management software
- **Barriers**: Needs audit trail (who approved what when)

### How OPTR Helps
- **Before OPTR**: 14 hours per compliance review, 5% miss requirements
- **After OPTR**: 2 hours per review (automated matrix), 0% miss requirements
- **ROI**: Prevent 1 non-responsive proposal per year ($30K wasted cost)
- **Win**: Sleep soundly knowing every requirement is covered

### Messaging That Resonates
- ✅ "Automated compliance matrices in 2 minutes (95%+ accuracy)"
- ✅ "Never miss a requirement again (audit trail included)"
- ✅ "Review 10 proposals in the time it used to take for 1"
- ✅ "DCAA-compliant documentation (export to PDF)"
- ❌ "Grow your pipeline 2×" (Carol doesn't care about pipeline, only compliance)

### Sales Playbook
1. **Outreach**: LinkedIn post in NCMA group about compliance
2. **Webinar**: "Never Submit a Non-Responsive Proposal Again"
3. **Demo**: Upload real RFP → auto-generate compliance matrix
4. **Trial**: 30 days, use on next 3 proposals
5. **Close**: Annual subscription ($18K/year) with compliance guarantee
