  P_win = 0.30 (30% win rate if responsive)
  M = 0.15 (profit margin)

  Non-responsive_proposals = 30 × 0.05
                           = 1.5 per year
                           
  Cost_per_miss = $30,000 + ($10M × 0.30 × 0.15)
