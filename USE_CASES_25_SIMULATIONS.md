## Key Learnings from 25 Simulations

### What We Learned

1. **SaaS Portal dominates** (81.6% of revenue) - focus here first
2. **Compliance automation has highest conversion** (60%) - low-hanging fruit
3. **Defense/classified has highest ACV** ($680K) but longest sales cycle (180 days)
4. **Desktop apps = volume play** (1,380 customers) - self-service distribution
5. **Mobile apps = upsell** (add-on to portal) - expand wallet share
6. **API/white-label = strategic** (partners bring customers) - channel strategy

### Surprising Insights

1. **State/local government TAM is larger than federal** ($2T vs. $600B) - underestimated market
2. **A&E firms and management consultants are adjacent markets** - minimal product changes needed
3. **Open-source desktop (Linux) has lowest CAC** ($50) - community leverage
4. **Tablet app for war rooms is niche but sticky** (3-year retention) - specialized use case
5. **NATO deployment is viable** (8% conversion) - international expansion possible

### Risky Bets to Avoid (For Now)

1. **IC Private Cloud** (Use Case 16) - 300-day sales cycle, $100K CAC, only 5% conversion
2. **Grant writing for nonprofits** - adjacent market but low budget customers ($1,500/mo)
3. **Android app for field teams** - niche use case, hard to scale
