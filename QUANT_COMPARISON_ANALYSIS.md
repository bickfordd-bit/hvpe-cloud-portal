#### Lesson 2: Quantitative Rigor Builds Trust
**What They Do**: Statistical confidence intervals, risk models, Sharpe ratios
**What You Should Do**:
- ✅ Already added 95% confidence intervals (good!)
- Add historical win rate predictions (e.g., "85% confidence to win")
- Add backtesting ("OPTR predicted 12 of 15 wins in 2023")
- Show statistical significance (e.g., "p < 0.05")

**Cost**: 2-3 days of development
**ROI**: Increases buyer confidence, premium pricing

#### Lesson 3: Infrastructure Scales with Revenue
**What They Do**: Start simple, invest as AUM grows
**What You Should Do**:
- **$0-500k ARR**: Use Vercel, OpenAI API, mock vector DB (current state)
- **$500k-2M ARR**: Add Redis caching, Pinecone, hire 2 engineers
- **$2M-10M ARR**: Migrate to AWS GovCloud, add Prometheus/Grafana, hire 10 engineers
- **$10M+ ARR**: Consider custom infrastructure, GPU clusters, research team

**Don't**: Over-invest in infrastructure before revenue. Renaissance started with Excel spreadsheets in 1982.

---

#### Lesson 4: Talent > Technology
**What They Do**: Recruit top PhDs, pay $500k-1M+ total comp
**What You Should Do**:
- **First Hire** (at $150k ARR): Senior full-stack engineer ($100k-150k)
- **Second Hire** (at $500k ARR): ML engineer with NLP experience ($120k-180k)
- **Third Hire** (at $1M ARR): Customer success / sales ($80k-120k + commission)
- **Fourth Hire** (at $2M ARR): Quant researcher (PhD in stats/ML, $150k-250k)

**Don't**: Hire PhD researchers before you have revenue. Focus on product engineers first.

---

#### Lesson 5: Compounding Knowledge
**What They Do**: Every trade refines the model (40 years of compounding)
**What You Should Do**:
- Log every OPTR run (predicted score vs. actual win/loss)
- Build feedback loop (customers report outcomes)
- Retrain models quarterly (improve accuracy over time)
- Measure performance (e.g., "Accuracy improved from 75% to 85% in 2024")

**Cost**: 1-2 days to build logging + feedback UI
**ROI**: Continuous improvement, defensible moat
