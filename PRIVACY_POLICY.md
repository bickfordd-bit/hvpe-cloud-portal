**Effective Date:** December 14, 2025

## Your Privacy, Your Control

Bickford Technologies LLC ("we," "our," or "Bickford") is committed to protecting your privacy and giving you complete control over your data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Bickford application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Intentions and Messages**: Text you enter into the chat interface
- **Account Information**: Email, name (if you create an account)
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with the application

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Technical Data**: IP address, browser type, device information
- **Usage Analytics**: Pages visited, features used, time spent (only if analytics enabled)
- **Session Data**: Temporary session identifiers for functionality

### 1.3 What We DON'T Collect
- We do NOT sell your data to third parties
- We do NOT share your intentions without consent
- We do NOT use your data for advertising
- We do NOT access your device camera, contacts, or files without permission

## 4. Data Storage & Security

### 4.1 Where We Store Data
- **Servers**: Secure cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP/Vercel)
- **Encryption**: All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Location**: United States (with EU data residency options available)

### 4.2 Security Measures
- End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Access controls and authentication requirements
- Automatic data breach detection and response

### 4.3 Data Retention
- **Default**: 30 days after last activity
- **Enterprise**: Configurable (1-365 days)
- **Deleted Data**: Permanently removed within 90 days
- **Backups**: Securely deleted within 180 days
