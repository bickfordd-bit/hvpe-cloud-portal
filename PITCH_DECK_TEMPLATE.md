3. **Visual Focus:** Use high-quality graphics and minimal text
4. **Confidence:** Demonstrate deep knowledge and passion
5. **Call to Action:** End with clear next steps

- Update company-specific references
- Include relevant case studies
- Add competitor comparisons
- Tailor financial projections
