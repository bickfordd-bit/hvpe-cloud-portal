## Implementation Guidelines

### Contract Negotiation Process:
1. **Initial Discussions:** NDA and option agreement
2. **Due Diligence:** Technical and financial review
3. **Term Sheet:** Key commercial terms agreed
4. **Definitive Agreement:** Full legal documentation
5. **Closing:** Payment and technology transfer

### Key Negotiation Points:
- **Purchase Price:** Valuation methodology and adjustments
- **Representations & Warranties:** Scope and survival periods
- **Indemnification:** Caps, baskets, and survival periods
- **Governing Law:** Choice of jurisdiction and venue

### Risk Mitigation:
- **Conditions Precedent:** Regulatory approvals, financing, etc.
- **Material Adverse Change:** Definitions and carve-outs
- **Termination Rights:** Specific conditions and remedies
- **Dispute Resolution:** Mediation, arbitration, or litigation

These contract templates provide flexible frameworks for different transaction structures while protecting the value and integrity of the Intent-to-Reality IP portfolio.
