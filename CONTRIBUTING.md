## Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing. This project is proprietary software.
By contributing code, documentation, tests, or other materials you agree to the
following terms and must comply with the contribution process below.

1) Contributor License Agreement / Assignment
- All contributors must sign a Contributor License Agreement (CLA) or execute
  a written assignment of intellectual property rights before contributions
  will be accepted. The CLA ensures that the project owner has the necessary
  rights to use and license contributions.

2) How to contribute
- Open an issue describing the change you propose.
- Fork the repository and create a branch for your change.
- Submit a pull request and reference the issue.

3) IP & Licensing
- By submitting a pull request, you warrant that you have the right to submit
  the contribution and that it does not violate any third-party agreements.
- You agree to assign or license the necessary rights to the project owner as
  required by the CLA.

4) Confidential information
- Do not include privileged, proprietary, or third-party confidential
  information in your contributions.

If you need the CLA or have questions, contact legal@your-domain.example.
