## Key Learnings from Simulations

### What Works

1. **Warm intros close faster** (Sarah: 15 days vs. Owen: 6 days with cold outreach)
2. **ROI calculators are critical** (all 3 deals required custom ROI models)
3. **Live demos with real data win** (Owen's RFP, Sarah's past performance)
4. **POCs close enterprise deals** (Steve needed 90-day proof before committing)
5. **Multi-threading is essential** (Sarah required buy-in from CFO, Steve from 3 execs)

### Common Objections & How to Handle

| Objection | Response | Proof Required |
|-----------|----------|----------------|
| "AI can't be trusted" | Show 95%+ accuracy benchmarks | Case studies |
| "Too expensive" | Show ROI model (100:1 returns) | Spreadsheet |
| "We already have a tool" | Show side-by-side comparison | Live demo |
| "Implementation is too complex" | Show 2-week timeline | Implementation plan |
| "What if it doesn't work?" | Offer money-back guarantee | Contract terms |

### Winning Tactics

1. **Always use the prospect's data** (upload their RFP in the demo)
2. **Quantify time savings** (60 hours → 5 minutes)
3. **Quantify revenue impact** (win 10% more deals = $XM)
4. **Anchor on industry benchmarks** (15% vs. 25% win rate)
5. **Offer risk-free trials/POCs** (remove fear of commitment)
